EV Module Health Monitoring System

Problem Statement

The global economy is transitioning from fuel-based to energy-based systems, with Electric Vehicles (EVs) at the center of this shift. However, a critical gap exists: there is no reliable, accessible system that accurately tracks and predicts battery health in real-time.

As batteries age, their charge-holding capacity degrades — resulting in reduced range, poor performance, unpredictable battery life, and significant economic loss for EV owners and manufacturers. This also indirectly contributes to increased carbon emissions and accelerates demand for rare earth minerals used in replacement batteries.


Our Solution

A software-based Battery Health Monitoring (BHM) system that:


Tracks battery composition and State of Health (SOH) over time
Monitors charging and discharging patterns
Analyzes driver-specific current drawing behavior and driving style
Correlates motor power delivery patterns with battery stress
Predicts future degradation before it causes failure



How It Works


Data Collection — Battery voltage, current, temperature, and SOC are continuously logged
Pattern Analysis — Charging habits, discharge rates, and motor load patterns are analyzed
Health Estimation — SOH and State of Charge (SOC) are estimated using algorithms
Degradation Prediction — Machine learning models predict capacity fade and remaining useful life
Alerts & Recommendations — System warns users of unsafe patterns and suggests optimal charging behavior

| Problem | Our Impact |
|---|---|
| Unpredictable battery failure | Early warning system |
| Reduced EV range over time | Optimized charging recommendations |
| High battery replacement cost | Extended battery lifecycle |
| Carbon emissions from manufacturing | Fewer replacement batteries needed |
| Rare earth mineral demand | Sustainable battery usage |
